How a size is chosen

  1. Need one core number. Perimeter, area or mean diameter. Best practice is all three from the same 3mensio systolic annulus trace.
  2. Hard IFU filters. A size is excluded if SOV min width is below the floor (25/27/29/31/34), SOV height < 15 mm, or ascending aorta is outside that size’s range.
  3. Range match. Score 0.45 perimeter + 0.35 area + 0.20 mean Ø, using how central the value sits in the published range. Out-of-range values score negatively.
  4. Overlap perimeters. 66 · 72–73 · 79 · 85 mm belong to two sizes. Both stay candidates. The higher score wins only if the gap is ≥ 0.08; otherwise the app returns a co-primary pair for the Heart Team.
  5. Tie-break penalties on the larger size (and flags on both): ellipticity < 0.73; min coronary height < 12 mm (−0.15) or < 10 mm (−0.35); SOV width < 2 mm above floor; STJ < labelled diameter; moderate/severe STJ calcium; severe LVOT calcium; access below FlexNav minimum.
  6. Consistency check. Perimeter-derived Ø = peri / π. Area-derived Ø = 2√(area/π). If these disagree with each other or with entered mean Ø by > 2 mm, the result is flagged — fix the tracing before arguing about 25 vs 27.
  7. Access. 23 and 25 mm need ≥ 5.0 mm vessel. 27, 29 and 35 mm need ≥ 5.5 mm. Below that is a warning, not always an exclude (alternative access exists).

Worked boundary rules

  1. 66 mm peri: 23 and 25 both legal on peri. Prefer 25 if area ≥ 338 and mean Ø ≥ 21 and SOV ≥ 27 and coronaries comfortable. Prefer 23 if area stays in 277–346, SOV is tight for 25, LCA/RCA low, or STJ calcium is significant.
  2. 72–73 mm peri: 25 and 27. 27 needs SOV ≥ 29 and access ≥ 5.5. If area is still ≤ 415 and mean Ø ≤ 23, 25 is usually cleaner.
  3. 79 mm peri: 27 and 29. 29 needs SOV ≥ 31 and AA 32–42. Do not upsize if coronaries are low or STJ is small.
  4. 85 mm peri: 29 and 35. 35 needs SOV ≥ 34 and mean Ø 27–30. 35 AA window is wide (27–44).

What this will not do

It will not read a blurry theatre photo of a 3mensio monitor and implant a valve. It will not replace virtual valve / VTC in 3mensio for coronary obstruction. It will not size bicuspids, ViV, or non-Navitor platforms. It will not store identifiable patient data on a server.
