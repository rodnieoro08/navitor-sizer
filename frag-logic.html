How a size is chosen

  1. IFU annulus diameter. 23: 19–21 · 25: 21–23 · 27: 23–25 · 29: 25–27 · 35: 27–30 mm.
  2. IFU ascending aorta. 23: 26–36 · 25: 28–38 · 27: 30–40 · 29: 32–42 · 35: 27–44 mm. Outside this range excludes that size.
  3. Perimeter and area are reference only. They support the size when they agree. They do not exclude a size by themselves.
  4. Annulus shape. Min/max diameter ratio below 0.73 is a warning (IFU footnote).
  5. Minimum coronary height is 10 mm. If LCA or RCA is entered and either is below 10 mm, no size is on-label.
  6. Shared edges. Annulus diameter 21, 23, 25 or 27 mm can fit two neighbouring sizes when the aorta also fits both. Both are shown.
  7. Access. 23 and 25 mm need ≥ 5.0 mm. 27, 29 and 35 mm need ≥ 5.5 mm. Below that is a warning.

What this will not do

It will not replace the IFU or the Heart Team. SOV, STJ and calcium are not IFU exclusion criteria in this build. It will not size bicuspid, valve-in-valve, or non-Navitor valves. It does not store patient data on a server.
